# Literature and law checked, 11 November 2026

Research agent's findings, condensed. Every claim links its source; anything
not confirmed is marked.

## The housing revision (RVLLH)

- **Law:** LF 2020 art. 146 ([Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042913635/)); experiment under LFR 2013 art. 74.
- **Timetable:** LF 2026 art. 106 ([JORF](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/article_jo/JORFARTI000053508773)) moves integration into the bases to **2031** (market reference 1 Jan 2028, declarations 2028, report by 1 Sept 2029, tariffs 2030); [DGCL calendar](https://www.collectivites-locales.gouv.fr/gerer-les-finances-publiques-locales/fiscalite-locale/fiscalite-directe-locale/reforme-des-valeurs-locatives). LF 2023 had already moved it from 2026 to 2028 ([Cour des comptes](https://www.ccomptes.fr/fr/documents/63540)).
- **Pending, not law:** a committee amendment (October 2026) to revise each property at sale from 2028; not passed as far as found.
- **Method:** sectors of homogeneous rental markets; €/m² tariffs per category and sector from declared rents (social and 1948-law rents excluded); localisation coefficients 0.7–1.3; tariffs set by the commissions départementales, the prefect deciding on disagreement.
- **Neutralisation:** art. 146 V.B: per tax and per authority, sum of unrevised housing values ÷ sum of revised ones at 1 Jan 2031. Each commune's housing base is fixed; individual changes are not capped. No statutory smoothing for housing (only a report on "dispositifs de neutralisation et d'atténuation", art. 146 VII). [IPP rapport 29, §1.2.3](https://www.ipp.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/29_revision-valeurs-locatives-habitation-evaluation-ipp-decembre-2020.pdf).
- **Professional premises (RVLLP, 2017):** in force with a neutralisation coefficient (CGI 1518 A quinquies) keeping each authority's professional and housing shares; planchonnement 2017–2026; a six-year smoothing of the 2027 update runs to 2031 ([impots.gouv](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/particulier/les-grands-principes-de-la-revision-des-valeurs-locatives)). Unresolved discrepancy on when the old tenths smoothing ends.

## What the revision would redistribute

- **DGFiP experiment, 2015, five départements** ([report part 2](https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/sites/default/files/ra/Le%20rapport%20de%20la%20DGFIP.pdf); [AMF note](http://medias.amf.asso.fr/docs/DOCUMENTS/AMF_24626TELECHARGER_LA_NOTE.pdf)): gross values ×2.5 for private housing (houses +185 %, flats +125 %). After rebasing, small units' tax rises (houses 65–74 m² +23.6 %, flats under 15 m² +35.8 %) and large ones' falls (houses ≥ 250 m² −32.2 %, flats 80–99 m² −14.3 %). Social housing at private tariffs +128 %; at social tariffs +18.7 %. No centre/periphery or building-age breakdown found.
- **IPP rapport n° 29, December 2020** ([report](https://www.ipp.eu/rapport-ipp-29-revision-valeurs-locatives-locaux-habitation-evaluation-sur-grandes-agglomerations/)): 20 large urban areas outside Paris, social housing exempt, market prices converted to rents, revenue held per commune. Pre-1950 dwellings +15 % (pre-1930 +20 %), 1960s–70s −16 %; centres over-valued today; poorest decile's dwellings **−5.6 %**, richest **+9 %**; large dwellings rise. (Conflicts with the DGFiP on size; the methods differ.)

## Rents and prices

- **Chapelle & Eyméoud, PLOS ONE 2022** ([doi](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0260405)), table 6: gross yield 6.29 % in rural communes (≈ €1,597/m²), 5.55 % in urban units of 200k–2m (≈ €2,356/m²), 5.24 % in the Paris unit (≈ €3,903/m²). Log-log slope of yield on price ≈ −0.2 (our calculation), so rent rises with price at an elasticity of **≈ 0.8**. Table 7: yields fall with size (one room 6.93 %, five or more 5.26 %).
- **Within a commune:** the IPP assumes a constant ratio (elasticity 1), citing little variation within communes; in Île-de-France the price gradient is steeper than the rent gradient (Bruneel et al. 2020). No within-commune elasticity estimate found.
- **Not retrieved:** Friggit (CGEDD) yield annex; INSEE or Banque de France series.
