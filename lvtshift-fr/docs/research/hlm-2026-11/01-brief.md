# Social housing (HLM) and the taxe foncière in a land value tax model (France)

A French civic model shifts the TFPB revenue-neutrally onto land value, per commune, from open data. Today the model treats HLM like any housing. The project lead has decided:
1. to model what HLM pay today correctly: 0 under a temporary exemption (CGI art. 1384 A and related), 70 % under the QPV abatement (art. 1388 bis);
2. that the reform ends these exemptions and abatements, so social landlords pay the land tax like everyone else;
3. that the change falls on social landlords, which are businesses (TFPB is not a recoverable charge), so it is shown as a landlord category and left out of the household income-distribution charts.

Please answer precisely, with sources, and flag uncertainty:
1. The current rules for 1384 A exemption durations (15/20/25/30 years) by financing type and date of decision, as of 2025. A simple open-data rule to estimate whether a dwelling is still exempt in 2025.
2. Does the commune's REI produit (the TFPB actually levied) exclude exempt bases, with the State compensation separate? How complete is that compensation today (partial versus the recent full compensation for approvals from 2021)?
3. Art. 1388 bis: rate, conditions, end date, and whether it applies to the 2024 QPV list.
4. Is the TFPB recoverable from tenants? We believe not.
5. For the site text: the strongest honest arguments, acceptable to a left-of-centre reader, that a land value tax should apply to social landlords' land, and that support for social housing should be direct and budgeted rather than through tax exemptions. Also the fair counter-arguments to acknowledge. Suggest a short French paragraph (5–7 sentences) for a civic site.

Be concise; rank uncertainties.
