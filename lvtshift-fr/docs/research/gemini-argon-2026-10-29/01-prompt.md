You are an expert in French local taxation (fiscalité directe locale) and land value taxation. Please critique a methodological change in a French land value tax (LVT) model. Be specific, cite CGI articles / BOFiP where relevant, and say clearly where you think we are wrong. Keep it under 800 words.

CONTEXT. The model replaces French property-related taxes with an equal-yield LVT. Scenario S1 replaces the taxe foncière (TFPB + TFPNB + annexes), the THRS and DMTO. Decision just taken: agricultural and natural land (PLU zones A and N, unbuilt) is EXCLUDED from the reform. It keeps paying today's TFPNB with its current exemptions, and takes no share of the LVT.

1. COMMUNE MODEL. The commune model was already revenue-neutral on the commune+EPCI TFPB product only (the TFPNB was never in the target; unbuilt land paid 0 today and a positive LVT bill). Now unbuilt parcels classified agricultural/natural from GPU zoning (A/N) — including parcels whose only buildings are farm buildings exempt under CGI 1382 6° — are given zero taxable value. Unbuilt parcels in U/AU zones, and unbuilt parcels with no known zoning, stay in the reform. Question: is excluding by PLU zone (rather than by cadastral nature de culture) defensible? Risks?

2. NATIONAL TOTALS. To avoid replacing the farmland TFPNB, we need the share of TFPNB levied on agricultural/natural land. No published statistic exists. Method: from the DGFiP REI (via OFGL), commune by commune, non-agricultural share = TAFNB base (B11TAFNB + B31TAFNB + B31MGPTAFNB) / commune TFPNB base (B11), applied to the commune (B13), EPCI (B33, with B31 as base) and syndicate amounts; the whole TAFNB amount counts as non-agricultural. Rationale: per BOI-IF-AUT-80 the TAFNB (CGI 1519 I) is assessed on cadastral groups 7 and 10-13 only, the mirror of the groups 1-6, 8, 9 covered by the art. 1394 B bis exemption; the TFPNB rate is uniform across groups within a commune. Cross-check: 2024 agricultural base implied by the 20 % exemption base D21 (D21/0.2*0.8) = 2 046 M€ vs 2 059 M€ by this method (0.6 %). Result: agricultural+natural = 1 032.9 M€ of 1 296.3 M€ in 2024 (80 %), 922.2 of 1 186.9 in 2025 (78 %; the 1394 B bis exemption rose from 20 % to 30 % in 2025, LF 2025 art. 66). The same farm share is applied to the non-built parts of the syndicates, TSE and GEMAPI lines, and the State's 3 % / 9 % fees (CGI 1641) on them. The ~131 M€ State compensation for the agricultural exemption is left outside (all State compensations are out of scope by an earlier decision).
Questions: (a) Is the TAFNB base a valid proxy for the non-agricultural TFPNB base? Any exceptions (e.g. tourbières, Corsica, DOM, communes where TAFNB is not levied, exemptions or abatements that differ between the two bases, the 1394 B bis base being net/gross)? (b) Is it right that the TFPNB rate is uniform across groups in a commune? (c) Is the national S1 change coherent: S1 falls from 67 113 to 66 140 M€ and the indicative rate (yield / taxable built land 6 091 Md€) rises from 1.06 % to 1.09 %?

3. SIDE EFFECT. Removing thousands of farmland parcels let a rural commune (Cahors, 9 IRIS) form five income "fifths" that it previously could not, publishing a fragile result (poorest fifth +11.5 %). We added a rule: income fifths need >= 10 distinct IRIS median incomes (two per fifth), else unpublished. Is that threshold reasonable for Filosofi IRIS data, or would you suggest a different rule?
